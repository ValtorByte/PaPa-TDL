Your responsibilities

You are responsible for your accounts, content, server administration, available storage and any provider charges. Access only groups, messages and media you are authorized to use. Respect copyright, privacy and applicable law, as well as Telegram's and Google's terms. Do not use the tool to bypass access controls or protected-media restrictions.

Credentials and sharing

Protect your Telegram session and Google tokens: they can grant account access. Do not commit them to a public repository or share the runtime data directory. Users who choose a shared server must consider who administers it. Check any sharing permissions before distributing links.

Availability and data loss

Third-party interfaces, authorization rules, quotas and networks may change. Transfers can fail or be missed; duplicate checks are not a universal content-deduplication guarantee. Keep independent backups and verify important transfers. There is no guaranteed uptime, response time, historical completeness or permanent access to an account.
